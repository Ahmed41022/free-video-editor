The Windows build of Video Converter includes unmodified libraries from Qt for Python
(PySide6, https://www.qt.io/qt-for-python), which provides the user interface and video playback.

Qt and PySide6 are used under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 (LGPLv3):
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html

The Qt Multimedia module ships its own FFmpeg libraries (avcodec, avformat, avutil, swresample,
swscale), which are also used under the LGPL.

Source code:
  Qt:       https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/
  PySide6:  https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/
  FFmpeg:   https://ffmpeg.org/download.html#get-sources

You may replace these libraries with your own builds: they are separate DLL files inside the
application's folder (or inside the temporary folder the single-file exe unpacks to at startup).
