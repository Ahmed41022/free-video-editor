Free Video Editor

A free video editor for Windows. Make videos for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, fix videos that won't play on your phone, and more. Everything stays on your computer.

Download for Windows Installer (opens the download page) Portable version Single file, no install

Free and open source (MIT). No account, no ads, no uploads. Windows may show a warning the first time: here is why.

What it does

Add your videos, pick a preset, click Export. Your originals are never changed: results are saved next to them as new files, like name (phone).mp4.

Editor

  • Video player with a timeline: thumbnails, audio waveform and drag handles to trim
  • Cut sections out of the middle; playback skips the cut parts
  • Join several files into one video, in the order you choose
  • A crop box right on the video: drag to choose what to keep, scroll to zoom
  • Before / after view that shows exactly what the export will look like
  • Undo and redo for every change, dark and light themes, drag and drop
  • Export queue that runs in the background with progress and time left

Video (MP4)

  • Reshape for phones (9:16), square posts (1:1), portrait posts (4:5) or widescreen (16:9)
  • Crop to fill the frame, or fit the whole video with a blurred background or black bars
  • Rotate, mirror, remove the sound; pick the size, frame rate and quality
  • Brightness, contrast, saturation, sharpen, noise reduction, and one-click looks
  • Stabilize shaky phone footage
  • One click for Play on phone, TikTok / Reels / Shorts, Instagram, YouTube and WhatsApp status
  • Phone recordings stored sideways are shown the right way up

Audio (MP3)

  • Turn any video into an MP3: 128, 192, 256 or 320 kbps, or variable bitrate
  • Keeps title and artist details
  • Also works on audio files (MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, FLAC, OGG, OPUS) to trim, cut or convert

Speed, fades and trimming

  • Trim to any range, and cut out as many sections as you like
  • Speed from 0.25× to 4× with natural-sounding audio, or play backwards
  • Fade in and out, with separate lengths for picture and sound
  • Never overwrites anything: existing names get a number, like file (1).mp3
  • Opens MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WEBM, M4V and other common formats

Video won't play on your phone?

Game recordings and screen captures often have a frame rate that keeps changing, and some phones just say the video is unsupported. The Play on phone preset gives the video a steady frame rate with phone-safe settings, keeping the whole picture and the sound. Convert a whole folder at once with Export all.

Step-by-step guide

Is it safe? Why does Windows warn me?

The short answer: yes, and here is how you can check.

Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time you run the app. That happens to any program that is new or not code-signed, because code-signing certificates cost money and this project is free. It does not mean a virus was found. To continue, click More info, then Run anyway.

It is open source. The whole source code is public on GitHub, and the downloads are built there by an automated workflow. Each release lists SHA-256 checksums so you can confirm that your file is the one that was published.

Nothing is uploaded. All the work happens on your computer, using the FFmpeg engine built into the app. It does not need an internet connection, and it has no accounts, ads or tracking.

Get it

The installer adds a Start menu entry, and can add a desktop shortcut and an "Open with Free Video Editor" entry for video and audio files. It installs just for you, with no administrator rights needed. The portable version is one file: double-click it. Everything the app needs is built in either way.
